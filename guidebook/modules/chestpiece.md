# Shield Modules

See [Modular Armor](../equipment/modular_armor.md) for more information about your Shield.

## <ItemImage id="draconicevolution:item_wyvern_shield_control" scale = "0.5"/> *Shield Control Module*

Shield Control modules let you enable your Energy Shield. (adjustable)

## <ItemImage id="draconicevolution:item_wyvern_shield_capacity" scale = "0.5"/> *Shield Capacity Module*

Shield Capacity modules increase your Shield Capacity, and also your Shield Recharge.

<ItemImage id="draconicevolution:item_wyvern_large_shield_capacity" scale = "0.4"/> Large Shield Capacity modules take as much space as four regular Shield Capacity modules, but provide extra Shield Capacity. In exchange, Large Shield Capacity modules do not provide any Shield Recharge, and have significantly higher Operating Costs.

## <ItemImage id="draconicevolution:item_wyvern_shield_recovery" scale = "0.5"/> *Shield Recovery Module*

Shield Recovery modules increase your Shield Recharge, and also your Shield Capacity.

# Other Chestpiece Modules

## <ItemImage id="draconicevolution:item_wyvern_undying" scale = "0.5"/> *Undying Module*

Undying modules prevent lethal damage. Once triggered, it gives a temporary health and shield boost, and enters cooldown.

## <ItemImage id="draconicevolution:item_draconium_jump" scale = "0.5"/> <ItemLink id="draconicevolution:item_draconium_jump"/>

Jump modules increase your jump height. (adjustable)

## <ItemImage id="draconicevolution:item_draconium_auto_feed" scale = "0.5"/> <ItemLink id="draconicevolution:item_draconium_auto_feed"/>

Auto Feed modules will automatically feed you when you're hungry. Food will automatically be removed from your inventory to fill the module's internal buffer. (adjustable)

## <ItemImage id="draconicevolution:item_wyvern_flight" scale = "0.5"/> *Flight Module*
Flight modules enable Elytra- and/or Creative-style flight. While gliding, hold **<KeyBind id="key.sprint"/> + <KeyBind id="key.forward"/>** to boost forwards. (adjustable)

## <ItemImage id="draconicevolution:item_wyvern_hill_step" scale = "0.5"/> <ItemLink id="draconicevolution:item_wyvern_hill_step"/>

Hill Step modules allow you to walk up single blocks, like a Horse.

## <ItemImage id="draconicevolution:item_wyvern_night_vision" scale = "0.5"/> <ItemLink id="draconicevolution:item_wyvern_night_vision"/>

Night Vision modules give you Night Vision if your surroundings are dark enough. (adjustable)

## <ItemImage id="draconicevolution:item_wyvern_aqua_adapt" scale = "0.5"/> <ItemLink id="draconicevolution:item_wyvern_aqua_adapt"/>

Aqua Adept modules remove your mining penalty while underwater.

## <ItemImage id="draconicevolution:item_wyvern_mining_stability" scale = "0.5"/> <ItemLink id="draconicevolution:item_wyvern_mining_stability"/>

Mining Stability modules remove your mining penalty while in the air.
