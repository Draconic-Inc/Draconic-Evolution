# Bow Modules

## <ItemImage id="draconicevolution:item_wyvern_proj_damage" scale = "0.5"/> *Projectile Damage Module*
Projectile Damage modules increase the raw damage of your arrows, but reduce accuracy.


## <ItemImage id="draconicevolution:item_wyvern_proj_grav_comp" scale = "0.5"/> *Projectile Gravity Compensation Module*
Projectile Gravity Compensation modules reduce the effect gravity has on your arrows, but also reduces raw damage, velocity, and accuracy.


## <ItemImage id="draconicevolution:item_wyvern_proj_penetration" scale = "0.5"/> *Projectile Penetration Module*
Projectile Penetration modules increase how often your arrows pierce through entities, but reduce accuracy.


## <ItemImage id="draconicevolution:item_wyvern_proj_velocity" scale = "0.5"/> *Projectile Velocity Module*
Projectile Damage modules increase the velocity of your arrows [(and thus increasing damage)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Arrow#Damage), but reduce accuracy.

## <ItemImage id="draconicevolution:item_wyvern_proj_accuracy" scale = "0.5"/> *Projectile Accuracy Module*
Projectile Accuracy modules increase the accuracy of your arrows, but reduce velocity and accuracy.

## <ItemImage id="draconicevolution:item_wyvern_auto_fire" scale = "0.5"/> <ItemLink id="draconicevolution:item_wyvern_auto_fire"/>

Auto Fire modules automatically fire an arrow when the bow is fully charged (adjustable)

## <ItemImage id="draconicevolution:item_draconic_proj_anti_immune" scale = "0.5"/> <ItemLink id="draconicevolution:item_draconic_proj_anti_immune"/>

Projectile Immunity Cancellation modules prevent mobs from teleporting away (or otherwise being immune) from your arrows.
