Draconic Evolution tools require Operating Potential (or Forge Energy) to run. Unfortunately, their batteries are not included when crafting.
Thankfully, you can upgrade them with [modules](../modules/module_system.md)! The amount of space for modules is dependent on the tier of your item.

Item-specific options can be adjusted by pressing **<KeyBind id="key.draconicevolution.tool_config" />**.
