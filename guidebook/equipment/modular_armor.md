Welcome to the latest in cutting-edge armor technology!
Expanding on previous designs, Draconic Inc. has completely reimagined armor technology. Gone are the days of wearing bulky metal plates.
This equipment is highly configurable with a wide range of [upgrade modules](../modules/chestpiece.md) available! The amount of space for modules is dependent on the tier of your Chestpiece.

| Tier | Grid Size |
| ---- | -------- | 
| §5Wyvern		| 6 x 5, 30 slots total |
| §6Draconic	| 8 x 6, 48 slots total |
| §4Chaotic		| 10 x 8, 80 slots total |

If Curios is installed, then your Chestpiece can be equipped in a Body slot.

# Shield

The main selling point for Chestpieces is the Shield, capable of absorbing almost any type of damage. Information about your Shield will appear when a Chestpiece is equipped, and displays the following information:

![Shield HUD](./shield_hud.png)

1. Your current Shield capacity
2. The cooldown before your Shield starts regenerating
3. Your current energy total across your Chestpiece and Capacitors
4. The status of your Undying Modules, if any
