# Mob Grinder

A centerpiece for an automated mob farm, able to kill mobs up to seven blocks away. Uses OP to operate, which can be supplied either externally, or with a [Flux Capacitor](../equipment/modular_tools.md) in its capacitor slot.

The <ItemLink id="draconicevolution:grinder" /> can filter what type of entity to attack, such as hostile mobs, tamed mobs, adult mobs, and by entity registry. Entity types can also be grouped into subtypes for more granular control.

If enabled, the Grinder will automatically collect items and XP. Items will be exported to adjacent inventories, such as a <ItemLink id="minecraft:chest" />. XP will be stored internally, and can either be extracted as a fluid (such as with EnderIO), or retrieved in certain amounts via the GUI.

The <ItemLink id="draconicevolution:grinder" /> can optionally be given a weapon to attack with.
