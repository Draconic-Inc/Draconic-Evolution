# Item Dislocators
<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
    <Row>
        <ItemImage id="draconicevolution:magnet" scale = "4"/>
        <ItemImage id="draconicevolution:advanced_magnet" scale = "4"/>
    </Row>
</Column>
Transfers items and XP into your inventory. The <ItemLink id="draconicevolution:magnet" /> has a range of 8 blocks, while the <ItemLink id="draconicevolution:magnet" /> has a range of 32 blocks. Item Dislocators are disabled while holding **<KeyBind id="key.sneak" />**, and can be toggled by pressing **<KeyBind id="key.sneak" /> + <KeyBind id="key.use" />** while in-hand, or **<KeyBind id="key.draconicevolution.toggle_magnet" />** while in the inventory.

If Curios is installed, the Item Dislocator can be equipped in a Curios slot.

# Dislocation Inhibitor

Prevents items being picked up by an Item Dislocator in a five-block radius.
