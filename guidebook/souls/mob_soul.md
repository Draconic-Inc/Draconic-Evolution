# Mob Soul

Mob souls are occasionally dropped my mobs when killed by a player using a weapon with the Reaper enchant. [Modular Tools](../equipment/modular_tools.md) essentially have a built-in reaper enchant equivalent to lvl. 1 for Wyvern, lvl. 2 for Draconic and lvl. 3 for the Staff of Power.

Some mobs will never drop their soul, such as The Wither, Ender Dragon, and [Chaos Guardian](../chaos.md).

The main use for a mob soul is setting the spawn type for a <ItemLink id="draconicevolution:stabilized_spawner"/>, but they can also **<KeyBind id="key.sneak" /> + <KeyBind id="key.use" />** on a block to spawn the mob within the soul, consuming the soul in the process.
