# Draconium

<BlockImage id="draconicevolution:end_draconium_ore" scale = "4"/>

Draconium Ore can be found in the Overworld, The Nether, and The End. It's most common in The End, and drops 2-4 (up to 12 with Fortune III) <ItemLink id="draconicevolution:draconium_dust" /> when broken without Silk Touch. When smelted, it can be used for almost every item in Draconic Evolution.

<ItemLink id="draconicevolution:draconium_dust" /> can also be obtained by killing an Ender Dragon or Chaos Guardian.
