# Chaos

<ItemLink id="draconicevolution:chaos_shard" />*s* are extremely powerful, and equally difficult to obtain. Islands imbued with such Chaos are rare, the closest known being ten *thousand* blocks away from the main End island.

**Make sure you are prepared, as once you step foot on this island, there is no turning back. At least, not before you defeat...**

# The Chaos Guardian

<ItemImage id="draconicevolution:mob_soul" components="draconicevolution:soul_id='draconicevolution:draconic_guardian'" scale = "10"/>

Chaos Guardian are Ender Dragons that have been twisted by the powers of Chaos, granting them devastating powers. Unfortunatly, they're just as hostile as a normal Ender Dragon, if not more.

When the fight begins, fourteen Guardian Crystals will spawn, shielding the Chaos Guardian. These crystals are also shielded by the Chaos Crystal in the center of the island, and must first be weakened by Chaos-inbued attacks before being destroyed.

The Chaos Guardian spends most of its time flying around, occasionally launching a barrage of Chaos-infused fireballs at you. When it approaches the center of the island, the Chaos Guardian will turn blue and become invincible, and then do one of three attacks:

- Launching fireballs at you
- Summon Guardian Withers (who unfortunately do not drop <ItemLink id="minecraft:nether_star" />*s*), and afterwards launch fireballs in a circle
- Charge up and shoot a laser at you that will tear through most blocks (and your paltry armor)

While the Chaos Guardian is attacking, the island may establish a gravity well, preventing you from flying and drawing you to the center of the island.

After all the Guardian Crystals have been destroyed, you can properly attack the Chaos Guardian. However, some of the Guardian Crystals may be revived by the island, so be careful!

Once you have defeated the Chaos Guardian, you can dig to the center of the island and claim your reward: The Chaos Crystal. Breaking it will drop five <ItemLink id="draconicevolution:chaos_shard" />*s*.
